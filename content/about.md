# Zhaoqing Xu 许钊箐

I am a **Ph.D. candidate** in the Department of Mathematics at the **University of South Carolina**, advised by [Prof. Xiaofeng Yang](https://people.math.sc.edu/xfyang/). I also serve as an **instructor of record** for undergraduate mathematics courses. My research lies in computational and applied mathematics — modeling, numerical analysis, and scientific computing, with applications to phase-field models, interfacial dynamics, and collective behavior.

> 好的数值方法，是在精度、效率与可靠性之间寻找平衡的艺术。

Born in Shijiazhuang, Hebei Province, China.

## Research Interests

- **Phase-field modeling & computation** — dendritic growth, anisotropic interfacial dynamics, energy-stable schemes
- **High-order time-stepping** — IMEX Runge–Kutta methods, spectral methods, unconditional energy stability
- **Scientific computing** — efficient decoupled solvers, structure-preserving algorithms
- **Collective behavior** — Euler-alignment systems, sticky particle methods
