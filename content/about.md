# Zhaoqing Xu 徐肇庆

I am a **Ph.D. candidate** in the Department of Mathematics at the **University of South Carolina**, where I also serve as an **instructor of record**. My work sits at the intersection of analysis and computation: I design and analyze numerical algorithms for partial differential equations, and I care equally about the theory that guarantees correctness and the code that makes it useful.

> 好的数值方法，是在精度、效率与可靠性之间寻找平衡的艺术。

## Research Interests

- **Numerical methods for PDEs** — finite element and finite difference schemes, stability and convergence analysis
- **Scientific computing** — fast solvers, adaptive methods, error estimation
- **Optimization** — gradient-based methods and their application to inverse problems
