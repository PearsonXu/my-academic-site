## Teaching Philosophy

> 教数学，是把严谨的论证变成可以看见、可以动手验证的东西。

Three principles guide my classroom:

1. **Intuition first, rigor second** — every theorem gets a picture before it gets a proof.
2. **Computation as a lens** — students implement small numerical experiments to *see* convergence happen.
3. **Low floor, high ceiling** — problems accessible at entry, but with room to stretch.
